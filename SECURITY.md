# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of **Hunty** seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us privately.

**Do not open a public GitHub issue.** Instead, please follow the process below.

### Disclosure Process

1. **Email** your report to **[security@hunty.dev](mailto:security@hunty.dev)**.
2. Include as much detail as possible:
   - A clear description of the vulnerability
   - Steps to reproduce the issue
   - Affected versions, components, or endpoints
   - Any proof-of-concept code, screenshots, or logs that demonstrate the issue
3. We will acknowledge receipt within **48 hours** and aim to provide an initial assessment within **5 business days**.
4. We will keep you informed of our progress and may reach out for additional details.
5. Once a fix is developed and deployed, we will coordinate a disclosure timeline with you.

### What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement:** Within 48 hours of your report.
- **Triage & Assessment:** Within 5 business days.
- **Fix Development:** We will work to resolve confirmed vulnerabilities as quickly as possible based on severity.
- **Public Disclosure:** We will credit you in the release notes (unless you request anonymity) after the fix is deployed.

### Scope

Security research is welcomed on:
- The Hunty web application (`apps/web/`)
- The Hunty mobile application (`apps/mobile/`)
- Associated APIs, smart contracts, and infrastructure

### Out of Scope

- Social engineering or phishing attacks
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies that have not been responsibly disclosed to the dependency maintainer first

### Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against security researchers who act in good faith and follow this disclosure policy. We consider good-faith research to be:

- Testing within the scope defined above
- Reporting vulnerabilities promptly
- Avoiding privacy violations, data destruction, or service disruption

Thank you for helping keep Hunty and its users safe.
